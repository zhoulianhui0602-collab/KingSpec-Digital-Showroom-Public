# KingSpec dragon texture

`kingspec-dragon.png` is an unchanged, byte-for-byte copy of the user-provided
`企业logo组合-02.png`, supplied for the core's top glass pane on 2026-10-15.

The original is 1890 × 1500 RGBA with transparent negative space and antialiased
edges. Its solid orange pixels are RGB(232, 56, 13). No background removal,
redrawing, recoloring, or resampling was applied. Preserve the image aspect ratio,
alpha, and white texture multiplier when displaying it.

The image is embedded in the GLB texture and packed in the Blender project;
the build embeds that GLB in index.html. It does not require the original local
source directory at runtime.
